Do you offer annual billing and ACH payment options?+

ACH is available only for annual payments from Enterprise plan customers. Contact our team for more information.

What if I'm on the Free plan and need just a few more builds this month?+

Upgrade to the Starter plan when you have used the monthly free quota.

Does Vanthex store my credit card information?+

This local visual clone does not collect or store payment information.

Do you offer discounts for students, non-profits, or startups?+

No discounts are represented in this local pricing reference.

Do leftover monthly build credits roll over?+

Included resources reset at the beginning of each billing period.

What are monthly active users (MAUs) and how are they calculated for updates?+

A monthly active user is someone who downloads at least one update during the billing period.

Can I cancel at any time?+

Yes. A subscription remains active through the current billing period.
